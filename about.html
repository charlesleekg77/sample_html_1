About us

A club built on
community.

Since 1985 we've been the place where Charlestown comes to play — a club run by members, for members, and open to absolutely everyone.

Our story

Forty years of sport for all.

Charles Sports Club began in 1985 with a single football pitch, a borrowed set of nets and forty founding members. Today we run twelve sports across our own grounds, and every one of them still starts with the same idea: that sport should be welcoming, affordable and fun.

We're a not-for-profit, member-owned club. Every subscription goes back into the grounds, the coaching and the next generation of players.
